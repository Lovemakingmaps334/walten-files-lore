Overview

The Walten Files is a horror series centered around Bon's Burgers, the employees who work there, and the horrifying events that unfold in and around the restaurant. The story mixes eerie surveillance footage, distorted memories, and unexplained torment to create an atmosphere of dread and paranoia.

The lore revolves around a web of trauma, disturbing experiments, suspicious disappearances, and recurring symbols tied to the company, the staff, and the dark truths beneath the surface.

Common Lore Theories

1. Memory and trauma are unreliable

The story's most unsettling feature may be that the truth is filtered through fear, grief, and damaged perception.

2. Bon's Burgers hides more than food service

The setting itself is likely tied to the source of the recurring horror, with the restaurant functioning as a symbol of routine and corruption.

3. The events are part of a larger cycle

Repetition, symbols, and emotional echoes suggest that the tragedy may be recurring or interconnected across time.
